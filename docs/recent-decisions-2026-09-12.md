Harfington/Uxcell material descriptions were seller claims. Epoxy-retained decorative rivets versus through-riveting remained proposals; no structural retention or matching patina was demonstrated. (R2, `a6c80b78-8b5d-4baf-b327-115861b5ec66`)
- **Forming:** tin snips were the builder's intended cutting tool. The assistant proposed forming to the actual curved path before rounding the edge, hidden relief cuts, and a short prototype containing both inside and outside radii. Suggested 4–5 mm cores and 4/5/6 mm comparisons are trial sizes, not a finished specification. The simplified arc-length advice omitted full bend/return allowances; determine the blank from an actual copper prototype. Annealing and permanent dowel support were suggestions, not completed work. (R2, `059aed54-a431-4d59-83c9-86bb08c147e4`; R3 final two turns)
